Synesis vocabulary syn:

Namespace: https://synesis-lang.org/ns/syn# · Turtle: syn.ttl · version 1.0

The Synesis compiler exports a project's organizing model (its ONTOLOGY blocks) as a lightweight SKOS ontology with EXPORT SKOS. SKOS covers almost everything; this vocabulary defines the two terms it lacks.

syn:counterExample

Subproperty of skos:note. A similar case to which the concept does not apply. Exported from a template field declared with ROLE NOTE COUNTEREXAMPLE.

syn:classifiedBy

Object property, range skos:Concept. Links a concept to a value of a closed vocabulary (an ENUMERATED or ORDERED field) of the project. Each such field becomes a subproperty of syn:classifiedBy, and its values a skos:ConceptScheme.
